Date night · Chinatown

Five tables made for two

The Chinatown date-night edit: rooms with warmth, food worth sharing and somewhere good to continue the evening when neither of you wants to call it.

By The After Six Editors 8 min read
The briefAtmosphere without theatre. Food that gives you something to talk about. A neighbourhood with an easy second stop.

Nearest stationsMaxwell · Outram Park · Chinatown

A good date-night restaurant does more than lower the lights. It creates an easy rhythm: a menu that invites sharing, a room with enough energy to soften the silences, and service that lets the evening take its time. Around Chinatown, five very different tables get that balance right.

Leila

01 / TECK LIM ROAD

Leila is the lively choice: a warm Mediterranean–Balkan room where generous dishes arrive for the table and dinner can naturally stretch into cocktails. The energy is sociable rather than hushed, making it a good fit for couples who want their date to feel like a night out.

Start with the house bread, keep room for something from the grill, and share dessert. On selected evenings, music and performances lift the room further.

Best forA spirited first or fifth date
Order togetherBread, prawns, kunafe
AfterwardsA slow walk through Keong Saik

Visit Leila’s website

Bar Cicheti

02 / JI TECK STREET

For pasta and a bottle chosen with care, Bar Cicheti offers an intimate, unfussy room. It feels special without becoming ceremonial—the kind of place where ordering another plate is an easy decision.

Esquina

03 / JIAK CHUAN ROAD

Seats near the counter bring the cooking close. The compact room, modern Spanish plates and lively pace work especially well for a date that prefers energy to formality.

Blue Label Pizza & Wine

04 / ANN SIANG ROAD

Pizza, wine and low light are reliable ingredients for an easy evening. This is the relaxed option for couples who want generous food and a soundtrack, without planning every course.

Restaurant JAG

05 / ROBERTSON QUAY

For a milestone dinner, JAG shifts the evening into tasting-menu territory. The experience is more composed, suited to an anniversary or celebration where dinner itself is the destination.

Venue details and schedules change. Confirm current menus, opening hours and events directly before visiting.
